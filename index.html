How BUDs works

  • Matched on your study plans

    You only see people preparing for the same exam, ordered by how well your plans fit: exam date, study schedule, time zone, daily study time and how you like to communicate.

  • Swipe, match and chat

    Swipe right on people you'd like to study with. When it's mutual, you're matched and can chat in the app to plan your sessions.

  • Built with safety in mind

    BUDs is for adults (18+) only. You can choose to see same-gender partners only, and report or block anyone at any time.
