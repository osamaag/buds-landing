BUDs ("BUDs", "we", "us") is a study-partner app that helps people preparing for exams such as USMLE, OET, IELTS and PLAB find others to study with. BUDs is operated by Muhammad Osama Bhatti in Lahore, Pakistan.

This policy explains what information BUDs collects, how it is used, who can see it, and the choices you have. If you have questions, email budsapp.team@gmail.com.

1. Who can use BUDs

BUDs is only for people aged 18 or older. We ask for your date of birth when you sign up and do not allow anyone under 18 to create a profile. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we will delete it.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your email address and password. Passwords are stored in a scrambled (hashed) form; we cannot see them.
  • Profile details: your name, gender, date of birth, country or region, a short bio, your study strengths and weaknesses, your preferences for a study partner (including whether you want a same-gender partner), your study schedule and your preferred way of communicating.
  • Exam profiles: the exams you are preparing for, and for each one your study start date, target exam date, study intensity and daily study time.
  • Messages: the messages you send to your matches in BUDs chat.
  • Reports: if you report another user, the reason you choose and any details you write.

Information created when you use BUDs

  • Who you have swiped right or left on, your matches, and who you have blocked.
  • Basic technical records needed to run the service, such as when your account was created and when you sign in.
  • Device tokens: if you allow notifications, a push token for each phone you sign in on, so we can notify you about new study requests, matches and messages. Notifications show the other person's first name but never the text of a message, and we keep a record of the notifications sent to you for 30 days. The token is deleted when you log out on that phone or delete your account, and you can turn notifications off at any time in Profile → Notifications.

What we do not collect

BUDs does not collect your precise location, your contacts, photos from your phone, or payment information. BUDs does not currently use advertising or third-party analytics tools. If this changes, we will update this policy first.

3. How we use your information

We use your information only to run BUDs:

  • to create and secure your account and let you sign in, including sending password-reset and account emails;
  • to suggest study partners who are preparing for the same exam, ordered by how well your study plans fit (exam date, schedule, time zone, daily study time, intensity and communication style), and to apply your same-gender preference if you set one;
  • to let matched users chat;
  • to keep BUDs safe: to review reports, enforce our Terms of Service, and stop abuse;
  • to fix problems and improve the app.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with advertisers.

4. What other users can see

  • Anyone signed in to BUDs who is shown your profile can see: your name, gender, country or region, bio, and the details of your active exam profile (exam, study dates, intensity and daily study time).
  • Your matches can additionally see your partner preferences, and can read the messages you send them.
  • Never shown to other users: your email address, date of birth, strengths and weaknesses, and your inactive exam profiles.

People you have blocked, and people who have blocked you, cannot see each other anywhere in BUDs.

5. Service providers we use

We rely on a small number of companies to run BUDs. They process your information only on our behalf and only to provide their service:

  • Supabase — stores the BUDs database (accounts, profiles, matches and messages). Servers are located in India (Mumbai).
  • Render — runs the BUDs server that the app talks to. Servers are located in Singapore.
  • Google (Gmail) — sends account emails such as password resets.
  • Expo — delivers push notifications to your phone through Apple's and Google's notification services. It receives your device token and the notification itself, which never includes message text.
  • Apple and Google — distribute the app through the App Store and Google Play, under their own privacy policies.

Because of this, your information is stored and processed outside Pakistan. We choose providers that protect data with industry-standard security.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or if needed to protect the safety of our users or others.

6. How long we keep your information

We keep your information for as long as your account exists.

When you delete your account, your login, profile, exam profiles, swipes, matches, chats and messages are permanently deleted. Copies may remain in our providers' backups for a short period before being overwritten. Reports you made or that were made about you are kept so we can deal with safety issues, but the link to your account is removed.

7. Your choices and rights

  • See and correct your information: you can view and edit your profile and exam profiles in the app at any time.
  • Delete your account: go to Profile → Delete Account in the app. This is permanent.
  • Block or report: you can block or report any user from their card, their profile, or your chat with them.
  • Other requests: to ask for a copy of your information or for any other privacy request, email budsapp.team@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law. We will honour those rights where they apply.

8. Security

We use encrypted connections between the app and our servers, store passwords in hashed form, and use database rules so that each user can only access the information they are allowed to see. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password that you do not use elsewhere.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above, and for important changes we will tell you in the app or by email before they take effect.
